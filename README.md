The Huawei MatePad T8 (codenamed "kobe2") is an entry-level budget tablet from Huawei.

It was released in May 2020.

## Device specifications

Basic   | Spec Sheet
-------:|:-------------------------
Platform | MediaTek MT8768 (Octa-core Cortex-A53)
RAM & Storage | 2GB/16GB, 2GB/32GB, 3GB/32GB (LPDDR3 RAM, eMMC 5.1 storage)
Shipped Android Version | EMUI 10.0 (based on Android 10, no Google Play Services)
Battery | Non-removable, 5100 mAh
Display | 8.0″ LCD, 60Hz, 1280x800 (189 ppi)
Rear camera | 5MP AF 
Front camera | 2MP FF
Connectivity | Wi-Fi only / LTE variants available
